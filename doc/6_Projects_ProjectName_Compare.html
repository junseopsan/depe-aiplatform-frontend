@@ §4.2 Flanges @@
All flanges shall be weld-neck (WN) type per ASME B16.5,
raised-face finish (125–200 µin Ra).
For sour service (C3A, A1A), flanges shall be forged A105N
or A694 F65 with full traceability (MTC 3.2).
+ RTJ flanges shall be used for Class 900 and higher.
+ Gasket materials shall be spiral-wound CS/Graphite.
@@ §5. Inspection and Testing Requirements @@
Visual inspection — 100%
PMI (Positive Material ID) — 100% alloy
Radiographic test (RT) — 100% sour service
Ultrasonic test (UT) — 10% CS butt welds
Hardness test — 100% per weld (post-PWHT) + Vickers < 250
Hydrostatic pressure test — 1.5× DP, 1 hr
@@ §6. Marking and Identification @@
All piping components shall be permanently marked with:
manufacturer's name, size, rating, material designation,
heat number, applicable ASTM/API standard, and NACE stamp.
