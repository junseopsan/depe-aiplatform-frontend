Design Criteria Specification
Saudi ARAMCO · Jafurah Gas Plant Phase 2 · Rev A
1. General Design Basis

This Design Criteria document establishes the minimum design requirements for the Jafurah Gas Plant Phase 2 Project. All engineering activities shall comply with the criteria defined herein, ARAMCO engineering standards (SAES series), and applicable international codes (API, ASME, IEC, NFPA).

1.1 Site Conditions
Parameter Value
Site Location Eastern Province, Saudi Arabia
Elevation 180 m above MSL
Ambient Temperature (Design) -5 °C to +52 °C
Relative Humidity (Max) 95%
Wind Speed (Design) 160 km/h (3-sec gust)
Seismic Zone SBC Zone 2A (PGA 0.10g)
Soil Bearing Capacity 250 kPa (allowable)
2. Process Design Basis

The plant is designed to process 2,200 MMSCFD of raw sour gas from the Jafurah unconventional field. Key design parameters:

  • Inlet gas H2S content: up to 10 mol%
  • Inlet gas CO2 content: up to 9 mol%
  • Product gas H2S specification: ≤ 4 ppmv
  • Condensate recovery: 425 MBPD (design)
  • Sulfur production: 4,000 MTPD (design)
  • Plant availability target: 98.0% annual
2.1 Process Margin Philosophy

A design margin of 10% above the nominal flow rate shall be applied to all major process equipment, piping, and utilities. Safety equipment (relief systems, flare headers, emergency shutdown) shall be designed for the maximum credible contingency case without application of design margin.

3. Mechanical Design Basis
3.1 Materials of Construction

Materials shall be selected in accordance with NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 for sour service. Minimum requirements:

Service Minimum Material Corrosion Allowance
Sour gas (wet) Clad / Overlay (Alloy 625) 3.0 mm
Amine service Carbon steel + PWHT 4.5 mm
Sulfur liquid Carbon steel (Cr-Mo) 6.0 mm
Cooling water Carbon steel + epoxy lining 1.5 mm
Produced water Duplex 2205 0 mm
3.2 Design Pressure and Temperature

Design pressure shall be the greater of (a) MAOP × 1.10, or (b) MAOP + 170 kPa. Design temperature shall be the maximum credible operating temperature plus 15 °C margin. For cryogenic service, the minimum design metal temperature (MDMT) shall be based on the minimum process temperature minus 10 °C.

4. Electrical Design Basis
System Voltage / Frequency
High Voltage Transmission 230 kV / 60 Hz
Medium Voltage Distribution 13.8 kV / 4.16 kV
Low Voltage (motors) 480 V, 3-phase
LV Utility / Lighting 208/120 V, 3-phase / 1-phase
DC System (control) 125 V DC
UPS System 120 V AC (min 30 min autonomy)
4.1 Hazardous Area Classification

Area classification shall be performed in accordance with API RP 505 and IEC 60079-10-1. Process units shall be classified as Class I, Division 2 or Zone 2 (minimum). All electrical equipment shall be certified for the applicable area class, with temperature rating T3 or better.

5. Instrumentation Design Basis
  • Control system: DCS based on Yokogawa CENTUM VP or equivalent
  • Safety instrumented system (SIS): TÜV certified SIL-3 logic solver
  • Signal standard: 4-20 mA HART (field) / Modbus TCP (inter-system)
  • Field instrument protection: IP 66 minimum
  • Control valve failure mode: per process safety review (fail-safe position)
6. Civil & Structural Design Basis

Civil and structural design shall comply with the Saudi Building Code (SBC) and applicable ACI, AISC, and ASCE standards. Foundations for major equipment shall be designed with a minimum load factor of 1.25 for permanent loads and 1.50 for transient loads.

7. Environmental & Safety
7.1 Emissions

The plant shall comply with the following emission limits:

Pollutant Limit (at stack)
SO2 150 mg/Nm³ (continuous)
NOx 100 mg/Nm³ (continuous)
Particulate Matter 30 mg/Nm³
CO 50 mg/Nm³
H2S (fugitive) < 100 ppmv (ambient boundary)
7.2 Fire and Gas Protection

All process areas shall be equipped with a fire and gas detection system compliant with NFPA 72 and IEC 60079-29. Voting logic shall be 2-out-of-3 for automatic shutdown initiation. Flame detectors shall be multi-spectrum IR type, UV/IR combined, or equivalent.

8. Schedule Constraints

60% Detailed Design Review 2027-03-15
