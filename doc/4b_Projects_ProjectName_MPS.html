Material Purchasing Specification (MPS)
Saudi ARAMCO · Jafurah Gas Plant Phase 2 · Rev A
1. Scope

This Material Purchasing Specification (MPS) defines the technical requirements for procurement of piping components (pipe, fittings, flanges, valves, gaskets, and bolting) for the Jafurah Gas Plant Phase 2 Project. This specification supplements ARAMCO Standard 02-SAMSS-011 and shall be used in conjunction with the relevant piping material classes.

2. Applicable Codes and Standards
  • ASME B31.3 — Process Piping
  • ASME B16.5 — Pipe Flanges and Flanged Fittings
  • ASME B16.9 — Factory-Made Wrought Buttwelding Fittings
  • ASME B16.34 — Valves - Flanged, Threaded, and Welding End
  • API 602 — Gate, Globe, and Check Valves for General Refinery Service
  • NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 — Materials for Sour Service
  • 02-SAMSS-011 — Procurement of Piping Components

4.2 Flanges

All flanges shall be weld-neck (WN) type per ASME B16.5, raised-face finish (125–250 µin Ra). For sour service (C3A, A1A), flanges shall be forged A105N or A694 F65 with full traceability (MTC 3.1). Ring Type Joint (RTJ) flanges shall be used for Class 900 and higher, or where specified by piping material class.

6. Marking and Identification

All piping components shall be permanently marked with: manufacturer's name or trademark, size, rating, material designation, heat number, and applicable ASTM/API standard. For sour service components, the marking shall include "NACE MR0175" or "HIC/SSC tested". Marking shall be by die-stamping (low stress) or vibro-etch; paint stencils are acceptable only for temporary identification.

7. Packaging, Shipping and Storage
  • All machined surfaces shall be protected with rust preventive coating (Mil-C-16173 Grade 2 or equivalent)
  • Flange faces shall be fitted with wooden or plastic covers
  • Valve ends shall be closed with plastic caps or plywood covers
  • Stainless steel and alloy components shall be packaged separately from carbon steel
  • All shipments shall include a certified packing list and material test certificates (MTC 3.1 or 3.2)
8. Vendor Documentation

The following documents shall be submitted with each shipment:

  • Material Test Certificate (MTC) per EN 10204 Type 3.1 (or 3.2 for critical service)
  • Hydrostatic test reports
  • NDE reports (RT films / UT records)
  • PMI reports for alloy components
  • Heat treatment charts (where applicable)
  • NACE compliance certificate (for sour service)
