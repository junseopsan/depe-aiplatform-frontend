ITB Review Sheet (IRS)
Saudi ARAMCO · Jafurah Gas Plant Phase 2 · Rev B
1. Purpose

This ITB Review Sheet (IRS) defines the technical and commercial information required from prospective vendors for the supply of process equipment for the Jafurah Gas Plant Phase 2 Project. All respondents shall provide responses in accordance with the formats and timelines specified herein.

2. Scope of Information Requested

The scope of this IRS covers the following categories:

  • Process design basis and performance guarantees
  • Material specifications and corrosion allowances
  • Equipment sizing and performance curves
  • Construction schedule and delivery milestones
  • Quality assurance / Quality control (QA/QC) procedures
  • Commissioning and start-up support requirements
3. Required Documentation
3.1 Technical Documentation

Vendors shall provide the following technical documentation as part of their response:

3.2 Commercial Documentation

Commercial submissions shall be separated from technical documentation in accordance with ARAMCO Standard Procurement Procedures (SA-02-001, Rev. 14):

  • Itemized price list by equipment category (USD, ex-works basis)
  • Proposed payment milestones and schedule
  • Delivery terms (Incoterms 2020)
  • Warranty period and defect liability terms
  • Spare parts list for 2-year operation
4. Response Format and Submission

All responses shall be submitted through the ARAMCO eMarketplace portal (vendors.aramco.com) in the following formats:

  • Technical volume: PDF (searchable, OCR-enabled)
  • Commercial volume: PDF + source XLSX
  • Drawings: PDF (vector) + native DWG / DGN files
  • File naming convention: [Vendor]_[DocType]_[Rev].[ext]
5. Technical Clarifications

Questions regarding this IRS shall be submitted in writing no later than 2026-05-15 via the designated project communication channel. Responses to vendor questions will be compiled and issued as an addendum to all participating vendors on 2026-05-22.

5.1 Evaluation Criteria

Responses will be evaluated against the following criteria:

6. Confidentiality

All information contained in this IRS and any subsequent correspondence shall be treated as confidential. Vendors shall not disclose any part of this information to third parties without prior written consent from the Employer. This obligation shall survive the expiration or termination of any resulting contract for a period of five (5) years.
